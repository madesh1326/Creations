How Hall Sync works

Pods

A pod is your group. Start one to get a 6-character code, then share the code so others can join from the same page. A pod of one works fine if you want to use the app alone.

Everything below (pacts, plans, focus) belongs to your pod, and everyone in it sees the same thing live. Leave pod takes you back to the start screen. Your pod and its data stay on the server, so you can rejoin with the code.

Creating a pact

Tap New pact. A pact is any promise your pod makes together. You can start from an idea (lights down, gym, water and so on) or write your own from scratch.

  • Name and emoji: what the pact is called and its icon.
  • The rule: a short explanation in your own words, shown on the card.
  • Repeats on: the days it applies. Days you leave unselected are rest days and never count against you.
  • Check-in opens and Check in by: both optional. Use them for pacts tied to a time of day. Leave both empty if it can be done at any time. Times stay within one day, so the latest deadline is 11:59 PM.
  • Streak style: Together or Personal (see Streaks).
  • Stakes: an optional forfeit, like "the slipper buys bubble tea".

The person who creates a pact can edit or delete it later. Deleting removes it for everyone !.

Joining and checking in

Pacts are opt-in. Tap Join pact on any pact you want to be part of. Once you've joined, the card shows your button for today:

  • Check in: tap it when you've done the thing, between the opening time and the deadline.
  • Opens at ...: the window hasn't started yet, so the button is greyed out.
  • Log late check-in: the deadline has passed. You can still record it, but it counts as late and breaks the streak.
  • Rest day today: the pact doesn't apply today.
  • Undo: after you check in, you can undo it on the same day if you tapped by mistake.

Under each card you can see everyone who joined and their status today: Done, Late, Missed, Not yet or Rest day. Leave pact removes you and your history for that pact.

Streaks

A streak counts how many scheduled days in a row were checked in on time.

  • Together: the pod shares one streak. A day only counts if everyone in the pact checked in by the deadline. One person missing, or checking in late, resets it for the whole pact, and the card says who broke it.
  • Personal: the pact is shared but each person keeps their own streak. Someone slipping only resets their own.

Rest days are skipped and never break a streak. Today doesn't break anything until its deadline has passed. People who join later aren't counted for days before they joined.

The row of seven dots on your card is your last week: filled green is done, amber is late, red is missed, a ring is still open, and a small faint dot is a rest day.

The Today ring

At the top of the Pacts tab, the ring shows how many of your pacts due today you've checked in on. It fills as you go, and the line beside it tells you how many are left. Late check-ins still count towards the ring, but not towards streaks.

Plans

Use Plans to put a hangout on the calendar. Tap a quick idea to fill in the title and times, or type your own. A title, date and start time are needed. The end time and note (where, what to bring) are optional.

Everyone in the pod sees the plan and can tap Join or Leave to say whether they're coming. You're added automatically to plans you create, and only the creator can delete a plan. Only upcoming plans are shown, so past ones drop off the list.

Calendar

The Calendar tab shows your pod's month at a glance. Tap a day to see its plans and your pacts for that day, and to add a plan on it.

  • Small squares are plans. An amber square means the plan ends after the hard stop, which is when your Lights down pact opens (10:30 PM if you have no such pact).
  • The round dot is your pact status for the day: green all done, amber a late check-in, red a missed one, a ring still open, and a dashed ring is coming up.
  • Show on calendar turns plans or your pacts on and off in the grid. The day details always show both.
  • Warnings appear on a plan when it overlaps another plan or runs past the hard stop.
  • Month summary counts the plans this month and how many of your pact check-ins were on time.
  • Jump to next plan takes you to the soonest upcoming plan. Arrow keys move between days.
  • Today's pacts can be checked in right from the day details. Past days are read-only.
Focus timer

The Focus tab is a study timer made of focus rounds and short breaks. Set your own focus and break lengths in minutes. The boxes lock while the timer is running, and your choice is remembered on this device.

  • Start / Pause: runs or pauses the clock. The countdown also shows in the browser tab title.
  • Reset: puts the clock back to the full length.
  • Switch to break / focus: swaps between modes without finishing.

A round only counts when a full focus round runs to zero. Pausing, resetting or switching early doesn't count. The Pod study board shows everyone's rounds and minutes for today, and a Focusing tag appears next to anyone with a round running right now. The board starts fresh each day.

Goals

The Goals tab is for your own targets. They are personal, so your pod mates don't see them in the app. Tap New goal, pick a quick idea or write your own, and choose how often it repeats: every day, or each week from Monday to Sunday.

  • Study time: set hours per day or per week. It fills up by itself whenever a full round finishes on the Focus tab.
  • A limit: stay at or under something, like supper out 2 times a week. You log each time yourself with the +1 button, and the bar turns amber at your limit and red once you go over.
  • A minimum: reach at least something, like 7 hours of sleep. Type today's amount and tap Save.

Each card shows your progress, the last 7 days (or 4 weeks), and a streak of periods in a row where you hit the goal or stayed within the limit. Edit changes a goal, and changing its type or unit clears what you logged.

Good to know
  • Changes sync across everyone's devices within a moment, as long as they can reach the server if its running !.
  • Your name and pod code are remembered in this browser, so reopening the page puts you straight back in.
  • The server has to be running for anything to save. If the page says it can't reach the server, start it and reload (Or just call Madesh).
